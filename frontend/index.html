My Devices

Link your personal computers, laptops, and servers together. Inference across your own devices is free — no credits used. Credits earned by any of your machines are combined into one balance on your main device.

This is different from the SwarmLLM network — linking devices groups your own hardware, while the network connects you with other people.

⚠ Only link your own devices. Linked devices forward 100% of their credits to the main device. Do not share invite codes with other users — this is for your personal machines only.
